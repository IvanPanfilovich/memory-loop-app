# Memory Loop – Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### 1. What is Memory Loop?

Memory Loop is a web app that turns long-form educational content (like YouTube videos and documents) into **personalized learning recaps**.

A typical recap includes:

- A short **audio summary** you can listen to  
- A **text summary** of the main ideas  
- **Flashcards** for active recall  
- **Links and timestamps** back to the original content  

It’s like building your own mini-course from the content you already watch and read.

---

### 2. How does Memory Loop work?

1. **Sign in** or create an account.  
2. **Add content**:
   - Paste a **YouTube video link**, or  
   - Upload **PDF / Word** documents.  
3. Memory Loop’s AI:
   - Extracts the transcript or text  
   - Detects **themes/topics** inside the content  
4. You **select the themes** that interest you.  
5. Memory Loop generates:
   - A **personalized text summary**  
   - An **audio recap**  
   - **Flashcards** based on key points  
6. The recap is **saved to your dashboard**, where you can search, pin, review, or delete it later.

---

### 3. Who is Memory Loop for?

Memory Loop is designed for anyone who learns from content, including:

- People who learn from **YouTube, podcasts and talks**  
- **Students** and lifelong learners  
- **Professionals** using courses, trainings, and webinars  

If you consume a lot of educational material and struggle to **remember** what you’ve learned, Memory Loop is for you.

---

## Content & Features

### 4. What types of content can I use?

You can currently use:

- **YouTube videos** (by pasting the link)  
- **PDF** and **Word** documents (up to **10 files** at once)

Memory Loop extracts text from these sources to build your recap.

---

### 5. What do I actually get from one recap?

Each recap can include:

- A **custom audio recap**  
- A **text summary** of key ideas  
- A set of **flashcards** for active recall  
- **Timestamps or source references** back to the original content (e.g., specific moments in a YouTube video or which documents were used)

---

### 6. Can I choose which parts of a video or document are summarized?

Yes. After you add your content, Memory Loop:

- Breaks it into **themes/topics**  
- Shows themes in a **timeline** (for YouTube) or a list (for documents)  
- Lets you **select or deselect** themes you care about  

Your summary, audio recap, and flashcards are generated **only from the themes you selected**.

---

### 7. How long does it take to generate a recap?

Generation time depends on:

- The **length** of the video or documents  
- The **number of documents** uploaded  
- Current **server load** and your **connection**

You’ll see a **progress bar** indicating what’s happening (e.g., “Analyzing content”, “Generating themes”, “Creating recap”).

---

### 8. What are flashcards in Memory Loop?

Flashcards are **question–answer cards** automatically generated by AI from your selected themes.

You can:

- Flip each card to reveal the answer  
- Navigate through them in a **carousel**  
- Use them for **active recall**, one of the most effective learning techniques.

---

### 9. Can I listen to the recap instead of reading?

Yes. Each recap can include an **audio version** generated with text-to-speech.

On the recap page you’ll find:

- A simple **audio player** (play/pause, seek, volume)  
- The ability to listen hands-free while doing other things.

---

### 10. Can I search my past recaps?

Yes. The **Dashboard** page includes a search bar where you can search by:

- Recap **title**  
- **Episode or content title**  
- **Themes/topics**

This makes it easy to find something you created earlier.

---

### 11. How are recaps organized on the dashboard?

Recaps are grouped into time-based sections such as:

- **Pinned**  
- **Today**  
- **This week**  
- **This month**  
- **Earlier**

You can **pin** important recaps so they always appear at the top.

---

### 12. Can I rename or delete a recap?

Yes. For each recap you can:

- **Rename** it  
- **Pin or unpin** it  
- **Delete** it if you no longer need it

These options are available from the recap card or recap page.

---

### 13. Can I jump back to the original content from a recap?

Yes.

- For **YouTube-based** recaps, you’ll see **timestamps** that open the original video at the relevant moment.  
- For **document-based** recaps, you can see **which documents** were used to create the recap.

---

## Accounts, Authentication & Profile

### 14. Do I need an account to use Memory Loop?

Yes. To generate and save recaps, you need to be **signed in**.

- If you’re not logged in, a **sign-in dialog** will appear.  
- After logging in, you’ll be taken to your **dashboard**.

---

### 15. How can I sign up or log in?

You can:

- Create an account or sign in with **email and password**, or  
- Use **Google** to sign in via OAuth  

There is a dedicated **Auth** page (`/auth`) for handling sign-in and sign-up flows.

---

### 16. What if I forget my password?

Use the **reset password** flow:

1. Click **“Forgot password”** or a similar option.  
2. Enter your **email address**.  
3. Follow the instructions in the **email link** you receive to set a new password.

There is a standalone **Reset Password** page for this process.

---

### 17. Can I change my display name or language?

Yes. On your **Profile** page you can:

- Change your **display name** (with validation)  
- Choose your **preferred language**

Changes to language are **auto-saved** and immediately reflected in the interface.

---

### 18. What languages does Memory Loop support?

Currently supported languages include:

- **English (en)** – default  
- **Russian (ru)**  

You can switch languages in the site header or in your **Profile**.

---

### 19. How do I log out?

You can log out from your **Profile** page or user menu.  
This ends your session and you’ll need to log in again next time.

---

### 20. Can I delete my account?

Yes. On the **Profile** page there is a **“Delete account”** option.

When you confirm:

- Your account is removed  
- Associated data is deleted according to the app’s data retention policy (see the Privacy Policy for details)

---

## Plans, Credits & Premium

### 21. Is Memory Loop free?

Memory Loop offers:

- A **Basic plan**  
- A **Premium plan**

Your current plan and credit balance are visible on your **Profile** page.

---

### 22. How do credits work?

Credits determine how many **recaps** or **AI operations** you can run.

On your Profile you can see:

- Your **plan status** (Basic, Premium, Expired)  
- Your **remaining credits**

Credits may **reset periodically** (for example monthly), depending on your plan.

---

### 23. How do I upgrade to Premium?

You can upgrade by:

- Clicking **“Upgrade to Premium”** in the sidebar or prompts, or  
- Visiting the dedicated **Premium / Paywall** page

There you’ll see:

- What Premium includes (enhanced recaps, more credits, etc.)  
- A button to **start or manage** your premium plan.

---

### 24. How do I manage or cancel my Premium subscription?

The Premium section on your **Profile** page shows:

- Your **current plan**  
- A **“Manage Payment”** button if you’re Premium (or your plan has expired)

Use that to manage billing, update payment methods, or cancel your subscription (exact options depend on the payment provider integration).

---

## Privacy, Security & Data

### 25. What data does Memory Loop collect?

Memory Loop collects:

- **Account data**: email, display name, language, country  
- **Content data**: YouTube links and uploaded documents (used only to generate recaps)  
- **Generated data**: recaps, summaries, audio URLs, flashcards  

For full and up-to-date details, see the **Privacy Policy** page in the app.

---

### 26. Are my recaps private?

Yes. Recaps are tied to **your account** and are visible **only to you** inside your dashboard (unless you manually share something outside the app).

There is no built-in public sharing feed or social profile.

---

### 27. Does Memory Loop use cookies or tracking?

Yes, with **user consent**.

- On first visit, you’ll see a **cookie consent** popup.  
- You can **accept** or **reject** analytics cookies (like Google Analytics or Smartlook).  
- Analytics load only if you **accept**.  

Your choice is saved (e.g., for 1 year) so you don’t have to decide every time.

---

### 28. Is the authentication secure?

Memory Loop uses modern, secure authentication and HTTPS (when deployed with SSL).

From a user perspective:

- Passwords are handled securely  
- **Google login** is available as a trusted option  
- Session tokens are refreshed automatically so you don’t have to log in constantly  

Always check that you’re on the correct domain and using **https://**.

---

## Devices, Installation & Technical

### 29. Which devices and browsers are supported?

Memory Loop supports **modern browsers** such as:

- Chrome  
- Firefox  
- Safari  
- Edge  

The UI is responsive and works on both **desktop** and **mobile** devices.

---

### 30. Is there a mobile app?

There is no separate native app, but Memory Loop is **PWA-ready** (Progressive Web App).

- On supported browsers, you can **install** it to your home screen.  
- Once installed, it behaves more like a **native app**, with its own icon and window.

---

### 31. Do I need to install anything to use Memory Loop?

No installation is required to use it in the browser.

- Simply visit the website and sign in.  
- Optionally, you can **install the PWA** version for a more app-like experience on desktop or mobile.

---

### 32. Does Memory Loop work offline?

Partially:

- You may be able to **view already-loaded content** in the PWA when offline.  
- You **cannot create new recaps** or run AI operations without an internet connection.

---

## Troubleshooting & Misc

### 33. I see an empty dashboard. What do I do?

If your dashboard is empty:

1. Click on **“Create recap”** or the **floating button** (on mobile).  
2. Choose between:
   - Pasting a **YouTube link**, or  
   - Uploading **documents**.  
3. Follow the prompts to generate your **first recap**.

You’ll then start seeing recaps listed in your dashboard.

---

### 34. My recap generation seems stuck. What should I try?

If it feels stuck for an unusually long time:

1. Check your **internet connection**.  
2. Confirm that the **YouTube video** is public and accessible.  
3. If using documents, ensure file types are **supported** (PDF / Word) and not extremely large.  
4. Refresh the page and try again.  
5. Try generating a recap for a **shorter** video or fewer documents.

If the problem persists, try again later or contact support if available.

---

### 35. Can I export my recaps (e.g., as PDF or files)?

Currently there is no built-in **one-click export to PDF or file** for recaps.

Exporting and downloading recaps is a planned improvement, so these options may appear in future versions of the app.

---

### 36. Can I share my recap with others?

Right now, Memory Loop does not provide:

- Public recap links, or  
- In-app collaborative editing

You can still share your learning manually (e.g., send notes, show your screen, play the audio recap), but there is no built-in sharing system yet.

“Sharing recaps” is on the roadmap as a **future enhancement**.
